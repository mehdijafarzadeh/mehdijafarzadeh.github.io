About

I work at the meeting point of language technology and language preservation. My research and practice centre on Kurdish — its dialects, its writing systems, and the digital infrastructure it still lacks.

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Freiburg, with an MA in Kurdish Language and Culture and a background in engineering and IT. I voluntarily coordinate the development of Natural Language Processing tools for Kurdish at the Mesopotamian Foundation in Amed (Diyarbakır, Turkey), building digital resources that support linguistic research, revitalisation, and accessibility — a spell checker, morphological analyser, verb conjugator and speech synthesis, all published free at nlpkurdi.com. I am also a member of Koma Kurmancî at the Kurdish Institute of Paris, where I contribute to vocabulary collection and the expansion of the Kurdish dictionary.

Alongside this, my professional work as a software engineer — data migration, backend systems, machine learning — shapes how I approach corpora, tooling, and reproducible pipelines for low-resource languages.

Kurdish Language Tools

Open, free tools for Kurmancî, developed with Weqfa Mezopotamyayê (Mesopotamian Foundation) and published at nlpkurdi.com. Interfaces in Kurdish and English.

Karkêş Verb Conjugator Full conjugation of Kurmancî verbs across tense, mood and person. Rastnûser Spell Checker Detects and corrects Kurmancî spelling against the standard orthography. Analîzkera Bêjeyan Word Analyzer Morphological analysis of a single word — structure, features, inflection. Analîzkerê Nivîsê Text Analyzer Runs the same analysis across a full passage of Kurmancî text. Deqxwîn Text to Speech Synthesises spoken Kurmancî from written text. Ferheng Dictionary · Kurmancî–Tirkî Searchable Kurmancî–Turkish lexical resource.
